# Twenty Eight Caffee — concept exploration

Status: three directions prepared for selection; no production site or concept-specific implementation started.

Inputs: the two supplied briefs and eight Instagram screenshots. The user's direct emphasis is the rustic, richly decorated room, distinctive objects and Vespas. The briefs request a concept choice before coding. These notes preserve that staged approach without treating research notes as customer copy.

## The room

The physical room is the identity. An intimate editorial composition uses cream, deep teal, plum and muted brass, with Fraunces and Manrope. A large doorway photograph overlaps lantern and embroidered-textile details. The Vespas appear with the exterior and arrival information.

Rhythm: photographic opening, dense material/room collage, continuous drinks ledger, compact visit block. Small persistent wordmark, plain text links and a directions action. The menu uses category columns and aligned prices only once current prices are approved.

Signature motion: one short sticky room scene separates photo layers and shifts attention between the room, lamps and textiles. Captions and practical actions remain stable. Use separate transform wrappers for scroll and pointer effects. Touch uses the same reading order and a shorter scene; reduced motion uses an intentional static collage. Full triggers, ranges, reverse behaviour and performance limits belong in design.md after selection.

Recommended: best reflects the supplied interior references without letting a single decorative object replace the café itself.

## The city stop

A colourful meeting place on Câmpineanu. Saturated teal, the existing sign's yellow, Vespa pink and ink, with Archivo Black and DM Sans. A large scooter-and-façade photograph, an irregular doorway image and compact blocks create street-poster energy.

Rhythm: street, threshold, drinks poster, address rail. An expandable shop-sign navigation keeps directions separately visible. The menu is one typographic poster with meaningful category anchors, rather than a grid of item cards.

Signature motion: a short sideways image reveal connects pavement and interior while vertical scrolling remains native. If approved source photography permits drink cutouts, they drift and rotate subtly before settling for menu reading. No fabricated cutout products or cartoon scooter journey. Mobile uses a short stacked reveal; reduced motion keeps both street and room visible.

Best if the user wants the Vespas and city energy to lead.

## The changing light

One place with changing moods. Cream and daylight transition toward petrol and amber, with Instrument Serif and DM Sans. One dominant photographic scene, a short supporting filmstrip, two connected menu spreads and a final visit panel. The scooter establishes daylight on the street; lamps carry the evening atmosphere.

Rhythm: cinematic opening, day/evening scene, coffee/non-alcoholic spread, evening-drink spread, visit. Quiet bottom or edge navigation. Menu spreads stay in the document, rather than hiding content behind tabs.

Signature motion: scroll coordinates light, image masks and typography over one brief scene, then releases to ordinary reading. Reverse scroll restores the previous composition. Mobile has a shorter transition; reduced motion shows adjacent images without scrubbing. Clean current coffee and evening-drink photography would strengthen this direction.

## Reference and content treatment

- Use the actual observed teal plaster, embroidery, metal lamps, painted façade motifs and scooters. Decoration alone does not establish a cultural origin story.
- Supplied screenshots are temporary internal concept references. Preserve source files; obtain clean approved originals and permissions for production.
- Vespas contribute authentic imagery. They do not establish scooter rentals, guaranteed parking, ride events or a universal club discount.
- Menu names in the comparison are illustrative layout samples. No prices, food menu, current promotions, booking system or unsupported services are represented as approved.
- Contact details and hours follow the supplied same-day research brief. Owner confirmation, menu approval, final logo, asset rights, production domain and legal copy remain launch inputs.
- Preserve “Twenty Eight Caffee” and Romanian-first copy. Text treatments in the comparison are provisional typography, not a replacement logo.
