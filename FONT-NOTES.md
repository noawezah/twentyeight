# Typography

Selected Eczar for display headings and the provisional typographic wordmark, Caveat 500 for handwritten photo notes, and Manrope for navigation, contact details and body text. The choice answers the user's request for a less generic font and the room's richly patterned, tactile character.

Eczar headings use 500–600 weight, upright emphasis, approximately -0.025 to -0.045em tracking and adjusted responsive sizes. Its irregular serifs and strong curves complement the painted exterior motifs. Caveat uses its actual normal handwriting design rather than a synthetic italic. Fonts are self-hosted by next/font; there are no browser requests to Google Fonts.
