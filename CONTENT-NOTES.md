This is an independent internal concept for Twenty Eight Caffee, not a commissioned or owner-approved production site.

The single editable content source is `src/data/cafe.ts`. Contact details, links and regular opening hours follow the supplied research brief checked on 22 October 2026. The business name intentionally retains the public “Caffee” spelling. Romanian copy is original proposal text, not quotations or an approved brand story.

## What the preview represents

- The actual supplied room references: turquoise surfaces, embroidered textiles, pierced-metal lamps, painted floral façade details and Vespas outside.
- Specialty coffee, tea/non-alcoholic drinks, wine and cocktail categories supported by the supplied research. Category entries are illustrative structure, not an approved current stock list. No prices, sizes, recipes, bean origins or allergens have been invented.
- A directions link, phone and email enquiries. No reservation system, booking guarantee or WhatsApp service is implied.

## Deliberate content omissions

The preview does not claim a particular opening year, founder story, cultural heritage, cuisine, charitable mission or award. Decoration is described through visible details. No food/brunch menu, private-event service, capacity, parking/accessibility policy, customer rating or live promotion has been invented.

Vespas contribute to the photographed street atmosphere. The site does not imply rentals, organised rides, guaranteed parking or a general visitor discount. Any Vespa Club benefit needs separate owner confirmation and eligibility terms before publication.

No forms, analytics or third-party embeds are needed for the concept’s discover → explore → directions/call journey. Additional production features should follow approved scope and an operational delivery process.
